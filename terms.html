What FilmCalc is

FilmCalc is a calculator that estimates the cost per photo of shooting film, using film and lab prices you enter yourself or import from the app's community-maintained preset library. It's a static, client-side tool — see the Privacy Policy for how your data is (and isn't) handled.

No warranty on prices or data

Preset data is community-contributed and reviewed on a best-effort basis, not verified against every lab's live pricing at the time you use the app. Always confirm the actual price with the lab or shop before relying on it.
