Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

FilmCalc is a static, client-side app. There's no account to create, no server-side database, and no backend at all — everything you enter (saved films, labs, prices, settings) lives only in your own browser's localStorage and is never transmitted anywhere. For the technical detail on that, see security.md in the source repo.

This page is about the one thing that can leave your browser: optional, opt-in analytics.

Analytics (Google Analytics)

FilmCalc may use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how many people use the app and roughly which parts get used — nothing more. It is strictly opt-in:

  • On your first visit, a small banner asks whether to allow analytics.
  • If you click Decline, or close the banner without answering, no analytics script ever loads. No request is made to Google, and no analytics cookie is ever set.
  • Only if you click Accept does the Google Analytics script load, from that point on.
  • You can change your mind at any time — Settings → Privacy has a button to reset your choice, which clears the saved preference and shows the banner again on your next visit.

If you do accept, here's what Google Analytics collects: which pages/views you use within the app, an approximate location derived from your IP address (the IP itself is truncated before Google stores it — see IP anonymization), your browser and device type, and how you arrived at the site (referrer). It sets standard Google Analytics cookies (_ga, _ga_*) to distinguish visitors across a session.

None of the data you enter into the calculator itself — film names, lab names, prices, saved profiles — is ever sent to Google or anywhere else. That data only ever exists in your own browser's localStorage, which analytics scripts have no access to.

Advertising

FilmCalc currently runs no advertising. An earlier application to Google AdSense was not approved, so no ad script runs and no ad cookies are set. If that changes in the future, this page will be updated first, and any ad network that uses cookies will go through the same opt-in consent gate analytics uses above — never on by default.

Other resources this page loads

A couple of third-party resources load automatically, regardless of your analytics choice, because they're what make the page work rather than anything that tracks you:

  • Google Fonts — the typeface files this page uses.
  • cdnjs.cloudflare.com — hosts the js-yaml library FilmCalc uses to read imported film/lab data files.

Neither sets cookies or receives anything beyond a standard request for the file itself.

Children's privacy

FilmCalc isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date at the top will change too. Worth a glance back occasionally if you're curious.
